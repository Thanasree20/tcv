Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a property valuation take?

Typically 3–5 working days depending on property size and location. We prioritise timely delivery for loan processing.

What documents are needed for valuation?

Sale deed, encumbrance certificate, property tax receipt, and any existing building plans. We'll confirm the full list when you enquire.

Do you serve areas outside Vellore, Chennai, and Salem?

No. We cover Vellore,Chennai and Salem in Tamil Nadu. Contact us for availability in your area.

How can I get a building plan approved?

We prepare building plans and estimates as per local regulations. We can guide you through the approval process step by step.
